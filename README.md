## 🚀 Walfi — B2B SaaS for digital loyalty cards

Walfi is a **B2B SaaS platform** that helps local businesses run digital loyalty programs through **Apple Wallet** and **Google Wallet**.

Customers don't need to download an app. The business scans a QR and the loyalty card updates directly on the customer's phone.

Live, with paying clients.

**I built and run the platform end to end.**

### Stack
`NestJS` · `Prisma` · `PostgreSQL` · `React` · `Next.js` · `Tailwind` · `Docker`

### What I manage
- **API** — 119 endpoints, 19 data models and wallet pass signing
- **4 frontends** — admin, merchant back-office, customer PWA and landing site
- **Database** — schema design, migrations and production changes
- **Infrastructure** — VPS, Docker, nginx, TLS, DNS, Cloudflare and CI/CD
- **Environments** — separate **staging and production** environments
- **Development workflow** — structured Git branching and controlled releases
- **Product operations** — Kanban planning and task coordination for development, sales and operations
- **Product** — development, deployment, maintenance and continuous evolution
